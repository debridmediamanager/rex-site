01 / What it does

The apps you know.
Nothing on your disk.

It looks like any other server.

Once you add it to your account it shows up in the apps next to your other servers. Movies and shows get posters and summaries and cast and seasons. Watched state and Continue Watching and playlists and collections and edits and Fix Match all work and survive a restart. Each file starts on the audio and subtitle languages your Plex account asks for.

Apps on your home network find it on their own when it runs on port 32400.

Many sources. One library.

Each source sends rex the files it has and keeps it up to date. zurg does this for you today and anything that can run ffprobe can do it too. A debrid account and a Usenet account each behind its own zurg end up in one Movies and one TV library.

The same film from two sources becomes one title with two versions.

It streams straight from the source.

When you press play rex reads the file from where it lives and hands it on. Nothing is downloaded first. Files play in their original quality. Delete a title in the app and it is deleted at its source too.

Give it FFmpeg and it also converts what a device cannot play. With a graphics chip a converted film starts in about 2 seconds even on a small Intel box.

04 / Limits

The things it will not do.

Movies and TV only.

Music and photo libraries stay empty. There is no Live TV and no DVR.

Away from home needs a Plex Pass.

As with any Plex server the account you watch with needs Plex Pass or a Remote Watch Pass to play outside your home. Converting video needs no pass.

Offline downloads lose text subtitles.

A file that plays as it is keeps its subtitles. When rex converts an MKV or MP4 the subtitle you picked comes along. The Plex apps for Android and iOS get a text subtitle as a track and every other app gets it drawn into the picture. Lines too dense to read in time such as an opening song's karaoke can be left out the first time. A subtitle you uploaded comes along too. A download for offline viewing still leaves text subtitles out.

It needs a source that probes.

rex never opens a file to learn what is inside. A title appears once its source has sent those details. zurg does this and a big library takes a few hours the first time.

Outside your home needs your own address.

The web app only connects over HTTPS and rex has no relay. rex-direct gives it one with a single port forward. Or give it a certificate for a hostname you own. Without a public IP at home use Tailscale or a small VPS that forwards to rex. Skip tunnels that cap bandwidth and keep a Cloudflare tunnel out of the way because it makes every play start read the whole file.

06 / Setup

Up and running.

  1. Download the build for your machine and unpack it. There are builds for Linux and macOS and Windows. Linux needs Ubuntu 22.04 or newer.
  2. Start rex once with its LAN IP. Nothing after this restarts it. The first start makes an empty Debrid Movies and Debrid TV library and names the server after the machine. rex never needs your Plex token or a TMDB key.
  3. Add it to a fresh Plex account made just for rex with a code from plex.tv/claim and share it with your own account. rex tells Plex its address the moment it is claimed and gets its own HTTPS address too. Do it before zurg sends anything so titles arrive with their posters. rex plays nothing until it is claimed, and this step is at your own risk.
  4. Add rex's LAN IP to zurg and restart zurg. It needs a zurg nightly from October 17 or later. zurg sends rex its whole library and then every change. With your optional DMM API key zurg also asks DMM exactly what each file is. Only this machine and your home network and Tailscale can push to it.
  5. Outside your home the apps want an HTTPS address. rex-direct gives every claimed rex one and needs a single port forward. Or start rex with a certificate for a hostname you own.
SETUP Linux · macOS · Windows 
# start it once (the first start makes the library)
rex --catalog rex.db --announce-url http://<rex's LAN IP>:32400

# claim it on a fresh Plex account with a code from plex.tv/claim
curl -X POST "http://<rex's LAN IP>:32400/myplex/claim?token=claim-xxxx"

# zurg config.yml
rex_url: http://<rex's LAN IP>:32400
dmm_api_key: <your DMM API key>   # optional: DMM's exact matches, else zurg reads the name

# outside your home forward port 32400 to rex
