01 / What it does

The apps you know.
Nothing on your disk.

It looks like any other server.

Once you add it to your account it shows up in the apps next to your other servers. Movies and shows get posters and summaries and cast and seasons. Watched state and Continue Watching and playlists and collections all work and survive a restart.

Apps on your home network find it on their own when it runs on port 32400.

Many sources. One library.

Each source sends rex the files it has and keeps it up to date. zurg does this for you today and anything that can run ffprobe can do it too. A debrid account and a Usenet account each behind its own zurg end up in one Movies and one TV library.

The same film from two sources becomes one title with two versions.

It streams straight from the source.

When you press play rex reads the file from where it lives and hands it on. Nothing is downloaded first. Files play in their original quality.

Give it FFmpeg and it also converts what a device cannot play. A graphics chip makes that fast.

04 / Limits

The things it will not do.

Movies and TV only.

Music and photo libraries stay empty. There is no Live TV and no DVR.

One person's view.

People you share it with see it the way the owner does and with the owner's watch history.

Converted video has no subtitles yet.

A file that plays as it is keeps its subtitles. One that rex converts for a device plays without them for now.

It needs a source that probes.

rex never opens a file to learn what is inside. A title appears once its source has sent those details. zurg does this and a big library takes a few hours the first time.

Outside your home needs your own address.

The web app only connects over HTTPS and rex has no relay. Give it a certificate for a hostname you own. Keep a Cloudflare tunnel out of the way because it makes every play start read the whole file.

06 / Setup

Up and running.

  1. Download the build for your machine and unpack it. Linux needs Ubuntu 22.04 or newer.
  2. Tell rex where your files are and give each source a token to send with.
  3. Make an empty library and start rex with a free TMDB key.
  4. Point zurg at rex. It sends its whole library and then every change.
  5. Add it to your account if you want the apps outside your home to see it.
SETUP Linux · macOS · Windows 
# make an empty Movies and TV library
rex catalog --seed --name myrex --out rex.db

# start it
rex --catalog rex.db --sources sources.json

# zurg config.yml
rex_url: http://<rex host>:32400
rex_feed_token: <the token from sources.json>
